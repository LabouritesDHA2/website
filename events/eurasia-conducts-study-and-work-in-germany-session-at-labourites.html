EURASIA Conducts Study and Work in Germany Session at Labourites

11 June 2026

EURASIA conducted an informative session on study and work opportunities in Germany, followed by a tour of Cadet College for Girls.

Labourites hosted an informative session conducted by EURASIA, focused on study and work opportunities in Germany.

The session helped participants understand the possibilities of pursuing higher education, career growth, and professional pathways in Germany. Attendees were introduced to useful guidance about international study options, work opportunities, and the steps involved in planning their future abroad.

The event also included a tour of Cadet College for Girls, giving participants a chance to explore the environment and learn more about the institution.

This session reflected Labourites’ commitment to supporting learning, awareness, and access to meaningful opportunities for students and professionals.

More learning. More exposure. More opportunities.
