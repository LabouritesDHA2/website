Labourites Hosts “Delulu” Stand-Up Comedy Show by RockBottom

13 June 2026

Labourites hosted “Delulu,” a stand-up comedy show presented by Rock Bottom, bringing an evening of laughter, entertainment, and community fun to DHA 2, Islamabad.

Labourites hosted “Delulu,” a stand-up comedy show presented by Rock Bottom, at Labourites Co-Working Space in DHA 2, Islamabad.

The event brought together comedy lovers for a fun-filled evening featuring an exciting lineup of performers, including Saad Goraya, Ibad, Suleman Sunny, and Mubashar Abbas, with Mamoon as the host.

The show created a lively atmosphere where guests enjoyed stand-up comedy, laughter, and a relaxed community experience inside Labourites’ event space.

Events like these reflect Labourites’ vision of being more than just a coworking space. It is a place where people can work, connect, relax, and enjoy meaningful community experiences.

More laughter. More community. More memorable nights.
