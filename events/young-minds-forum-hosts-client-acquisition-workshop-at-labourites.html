Young Minds Forum Hosts Client Acquisition Workshop at Labourites

13 June 2026

Young Minds Forum organized a practical workshop at Labourites on client acquisition for freelancers and agencies in the AI era.

Labourites hosted a practical workshop organized by Young Minds Forum on “Client Acquisition for Freelancers & Agencies in the AI Era.”

The session was led by Abdullah Ahmad, Co-founder of DevNauts, and focused on helping freelancers, agency owners, and professionals understand how to attract and convert clients in a changing digital landscape.

Participants learned about inbound and outbound client acquisition, positioning, creating better offers, LinkedIn profile and content optimization, lead generation through communities and networks, and common mistakes in profiles, proposals, and outreach.

The workshop brought together aspiring freelancers, agency owners, and professionals for a hands-on learning experience filled with practical insights and real examples.

Labourites is proud to support learning-focused community events that help professionals grow, connect, and build better opportunities.

More skills. More clients. More growth.
