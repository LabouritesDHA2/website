Young Minds Forum Hosts Meetup for Agency Owners and Freelancers

2 May 2026

Young Minds Forum organized a meetup at Labourites for agency owners and freelancers, bringing together professionals for networking, learning, and collaboration.

Labourites hosted an engaging meetup organized by Young Minds Forum, focused on bringing together agency owners, freelancers, entrepreneurs, and professionals under one roof.

The meetup created a valuable space for participants to connect, share ideas, discuss opportunities, and learn from each other’s experiences in the freelance and agency ecosystem.

The event was made even more impactful with the presence of Hisham Sarwar, Founder & CEO of Innovista, who joined the gathering and added great value to the session.

With many participants attending, the meetup reflected the growing need for strong professional communities, meaningful networking, and collaborative spaces where people can grow together.

Labourites is proud to support such community-driven events that empower freelancers, agency owners, and business professionals.

More connections. More collaboration. More growth.
